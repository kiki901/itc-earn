Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use ITC Earn, we collect the following information stored locally on your device:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and encrypted password
  • App Data: Your ITC balance, transactions, animal collections, and staking records

Note: All data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We do not collect or transmit any data to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the ITC Earn service
  • Process transactions and manage your virtual currency balance
  • Improve user experience and app functionality
  • Send important updates and notifications
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary to protect our rights or safety
  • With your explicit consent

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage using SHA-256 hashing
  • Secure local data storage
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request data deletion at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of non-essential data collection

7. Children's Privacy

ITC Earn is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.
